# SA17 — sourcing evidence for the acquisition registry

This is the evidence note that [source_instruments.toml](source_instruments.toml)
cites in its header. The registry was declared on 2026-11-02, before any
ingestion; this note was missing from the working tree. It was **written on
2026-11-07**, after every issuer page the registry cites was consulted again
that day. The note keeps the file name the registry gives it, so the
registry's own reference resolves. Nothing in the registry was changed.

It records identities and listing dates. It is **not** a frozen strategy
universe and not an admission to the SA17 study: the proposal stays
`PROPOSED / NOT_TESTED`, and the [data audit](data_audit.md)'s
`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` still stands for the 1999–2018 question.

## What was checked, and how

For each of the ten funds, the issuer's product page cited in the registry
(State Street Global Advisors, `ssga.com`) was read on 2026-11-07. Five fields
were taken from it: the fund name, ISIN, income treatment, replication and
current benchmark. The fund inception date and the full listing table
(exchange, trading currency, listing date, exchange ticker) were taken too.
The extraction excluded performance tables, and no performance figure was
read in this check. Earlier exposure to issuer performance tables is recorded
in the data audit.

## Limits that remain

- **The benchmark named above is today's.** The issuer pages give the current
  35/20 capped indices. The communication services page says its index was
  renamed from 25/20 to 35/20 on 24 March 2025. State Street's 2019
  shareholder notice, cited in the data audit, describes a switch to daily
  capped 25/20 indices from 15 March 2019. A dated benchmark history for each
  fund has not been assembled. Current names are not backdated.
- **The technology fund's scope changed in 2018.** The GICS revision of
  September 2018 moved companies from technology to communication services
  (data audit, *Sector and vehicle evidence*). Before ZPDK lists, the
  technology fund does not hold the exposure it holds now, and nothing here
  splits that out.
- **Real estate is missing.** The Invesco fund (`IE00BYM8JD58`) has no EUR
  listing established here. The SPDR real-estate ISIN (`IE00BFY0GR99`) has
  only a pending-launch KID. Neither is in the registry.
- **Survivorship is not resolved.** These are the funds discoverable today.
  Closed, merged or delisted UCITS sector funds that an investor could have
  held since 2015 have not been searched for. A selection rule fixed without
  them is still open.
- **The provider symbol is not issuer evidence.** The registry's Yahoo
  symbols (`ZPDE.DE` and so on) follow the issuer's exchange tickers, and the
  Reuters codes shown on the pages use the same `.DE` suffix (`ZPDM.DE`,
  `ZPDD.DE`, `ZPDK.DE`). Whether Yahoo serves each symbol from the listing
  date is known only once `scripts/SA17_download_data.py` has run, and its
  coverage report says it.
- **No history before the listing.** These are exchange quotations, not
  indices: no fund here trades before 2015-07-08 (2018-08-16 for
  communication services). Under the proposal's 36-month estimation, the
  first month-end closes are July 2015 and August 2018. The earliest decision
  with a full window is therefore the end of July 2018 for nine sectors and
  the end of August 2021 for communication services. That is arithmetic on
  listing dates, not observed coverage. These funds cannot serve the
  1999–2018 study.
